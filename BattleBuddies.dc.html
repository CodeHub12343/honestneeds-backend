How requesting assistance works

1 Tell us what you need

A short request — amount, deadline, what the money or item is for. No long essays.

2 Verify as a veteran once

DD-214 upload (encrypted, viewed by HonestNeed admins only). Takes under 48 hours.

3 Your Buddy Captain reviews

A trained peer helps shape the request and spots adjacent resources (VA, local nonprofits).

4 The community funds it

Payments go directly to the vendor or landlord — rent, utility, mechanic — not to a general fund.
