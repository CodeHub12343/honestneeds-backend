If You Need Help Right Now

You are not alone. Help is one call away.

The Veterans Crisis Line connects veterans in crisis — and their families — with qualified responders. 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. You don't need to be enrolled in VA benefits or healthcare.

Call 988 Then press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line

If you are in immediate physical danger, call 911.Battle Buddies are peers — not emergency services.

What happens when you reach out

A trained responder answers

Many are veterans themselves. They listen, assess, and help you figure out next steps — at your pace, in your words.

Confidential by default

You decide what to share. The responder's goal is your safety, not a report. VA enrollment is not required.

A plan, not just a call

Together you'll find resources — crisis clinicians, local VA care, warm handoffs, or follow-up — that match where you are.
