How Battle Buddies works

Four steps between standing alone and standing together.

Tell us what you're looking for

Someone to talk to, a workout partner, a mentor, a group — your call. No forms you can't finish.

Discover compatible people

Matches on location, branch, interests, and the kind of friendship you're after — not a swipe app.

03

Connect safely

Message in-app first. Verified veterans wear a badge. Report, block, or step back — any time, no explanation needed.

04

Build real community

Grab coffee. Work out together. Join a cookout. Keep showing up. That's how no veteran stands alone.

Our mission

No Veteran Stands Alone.

In the military you learned to watch out for the person beside you. We believe that principle should continue long after someone takes off the uniform.

Read the full mission

Our mission is simple but urgent: to make sure no veteran has to face life's hardest moments alone.

Sometimes

someone needs a person to talk to.

Sometimes

they need someone to grab a cup of coffee with.

Sometimes

they need another veteran who understands where they've been.

Sometimes

they need a group of people reminding them that they still belong.

"Our promise: a community where asking for connection is a sign of strength, where veterans build meaningful relationships before reaching a breaking point, and where someone struggling always has somewhere to turn."

— The Battle Buddies for Vets team, powered by HonestNeed Share our mission →
